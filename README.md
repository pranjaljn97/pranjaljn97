### About

I build **reliable, low-latency data systems for crypto intelligence** — ingestion, ETL, and analytics at scale.

Backend and data infrastructure is my home turf: streaming pipelines, schema design, and the unglamorous production reliability work that keeps them honest. Currently on the **Core Ingestion** team at [TRM Labs](https://www.trmlabs.com/) (blockchain intelligence), where I own ingestion reliability SLAs for on-chain/off-chain sources and exchange feeds. Previously led Data Engineering at **Postman**.

Outside the day job I spend time on **AI agents**, **market microstructure**, and whatever else earns a `git init`.

- 🔭 Working on streaming ingestion with back-pressure control, idempotent sinks, and schema evolution
- 🌱 Currently deep in **LLM agent orchestration** (LangGraph, browser agents) and **event-driven trading systems**
- 💬 Ask me about **Kafka, Spark, Airflow, schema registries, or why your pipeline is silently dropping rows**
- ⚡ Fitness nerd, AI enthusiast, and long-term markets observer
